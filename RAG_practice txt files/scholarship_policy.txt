The college may provide scholarships to eligible students according to academic performance and applicable scholarship schemes.

Eligibility requirements may differ depending on the scholarship category.

Students applying for scholarships may need to submit academic records, identity documents, income certificates, or other supporting documents.

Scholarship applications must be submitted before the deadline announced by the college.

Students are responsible for providing accurate information in their scholarship applications.

Providing false or misleading information may result in cancellation of the scholarship application.

Scholarship approval is subject to verification and applicable college or government rules.
