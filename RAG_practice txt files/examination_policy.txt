Students must carry their valid college identity card during examinations.

Students should reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled examination time.

Mobile phones, smart watches, wireless earphones, and other unauthorized electronic devices are not permitted inside the examination hall.

Students must follow the instructions given by the examination invigilator.

Students arriving late may be allowed to enter the examination hall only according to the examination rules.

Students should carry the required stationery and examination materials.

Students must not communicate with other students during the examination.

Any unfair means or prohibited activity during an examination may result in disciplinary action according to college regulations.
