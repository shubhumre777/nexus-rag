Students can issue a maximum of three books at a time from the college library.

Books are normally issued for a period of fourteen days.

Students should return borrowed books on or before the due date.

A late fee may be charged for overdue books according to library regulations.

Reference books may have different borrowing restrictions and may not be issued for normal home use.

Students must carry their valid college identity card when borrowing books.

Lost or damaged books may require replacement or payment according to library rules.

Students should maintain silence and proper discipline inside the library.
