Students must complete the academic requirements specified for their program and semester.

Students should regularly attend lectures, practical sessions, laboratories, and other academic activities.

Students must complete assignments, laboratory work, projects, and examinations within the deadlines specified by the department.

Students who fail to satisfy required academic conditions may face academic consequences according to institutional regulations.

Students should communicate with their faculty members when they face difficulties completing academic requirements.

The academic department may publish additional rules for specific courses or programs.

Students are expected to maintain academic discipline and follow instructions issued by the department.
