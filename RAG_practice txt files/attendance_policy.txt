Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in each course during the semester.

Students whose attendance falls below 75 percent may be detained from appearing in the final semester examinations.

Students with attendance between 65 percent and 75 percent may apply for attendance condonation if they have valid supporting reasons.

Attendance condonation is not automatic and approval depends on the college authorities.

Students are responsible for regularly checking their attendance records.

If a student believes that attendance has been recorded incorrectly, the student should contact the concerned faculty member or department office.

Attendance requirements apply separately to each course unless otherwise specified by the academic department.
