Students staying in the college hostel must follow the hostel entry and exit regulations.

Students must register their entry and exit when required by hostel authorities.

Visitors are allowed only during designated visiting hours.

Students must maintain cleanliness and discipline within hostel premises.

Ragging, harassment, violence, and other prohibited activities are not permitted in the hostel.

Students must not damage hostel property.

Students may be required to obtain permission before staying outside the hostel overnight.

Hostel residents must follow the specific timings and instructions communicated by the hostel administration.
