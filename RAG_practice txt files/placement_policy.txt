PLACEMENT POLICY

Students participating in campus placement activities must follow the eligibility requirements specified for each company.

Companies may define minimum academic requirements such as percentage, CGPA, number of active backlogs, or graduation status.

Students should provide accurate information in their placement applications and resumes.

Students selected for a company may be required to participate in further recruitment rounds.

Students should attend scheduled placement tests, interviews, and recruitment activities on time.

A student who accepts an offer may be required to follow the placement cell's applicable rules regarding participation in other recruitment processes.

Placement opportunities depend on the companies participating in the campus recruitment process.
